09 / Scientific methodology
Frontogenesis is estimated from horizontal temperature gradients and horizontal wind deformation at 850 hPa. This is a simplified kinematic approximation, not a complete three-dimensional thermodynamic frontogenesis calculation.
Finite differences estimate horizontal temperature gradients and wind deformation. Results are sensitive to sampling interval, missing values and the native weather model resolution.
