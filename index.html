09 / Scientific methodology
Frontogenesis is estimated from horizontal temperature gradients and horizontal wind deformation at 850 hPa. The diagnostic is a simplified kinematic approximation, not a complete three-dimensional thermodynamic frontogenesis calculation.
Temperature gradient is calculated from neighbouring grid points. Wind speed and direction are converted to horizontal wind components. Finite differences are used to estimate horizontal deformation. Values are sensitive to grid spacing, missing data and model resolution.
